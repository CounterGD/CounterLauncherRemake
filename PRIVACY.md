# CounterLauncher Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 31, 2026

CounterLauncher respects your privacy.

## Information We Collect

CounterLauncher does not intentionally collect personal information.

## Local Data

CounterLauncher stores information locally on your device, including:

- Launcher settings
- Downloaded Minecraft files
- Java runtimes
- Game assets
- Log files

This information remains on your device unless you choose to share it.

## Internet Connections

CounterLauncher connects to the Internet only when necessary to:

- Download Minecraft versions
- Download libraries
- Download assets
- Download Java runtimes
- Check for launcher updates (if enabled)

## Telemetry

CounterLauncher does not collect analytics or telemetry.

## Third-Party Services

CounterLauncher may download files from services operated by third parties, including:

- Mojang Studios
- Microsoft
- Java runtime providers

Those services are governed by their own privacy policies and terms of service.

## Security

Reasonable efforts are made to ensure downloaded files are obtained from trusted sources. However, no software can guarantee absolute security.

## Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated in future releases.

## Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be submitted through the CounterLauncher GitHub repository or other official CounterLauncher support channels.
